# MEMBRA Executive Summary

## One-Line Thesis

MEMBRA turns idle physical reality into permissioned, verified, local economic inventory.

## What MEMBRA Is

MEMBRA is a proof-of-reality monetization network. It converts real-world surfaces, household assets, wearable media, local handoff capacity, QR/NFC interactions, and proof events into structured records that can be listed, matched, verified, reported, and made eligible for settlement through external payment rails.

## First Commercial Wedge

The first wedge is proof-based physical media:

1. advertiser funds a local physical campaign
2. owner registers a surface or wearable placement
3. MEMBRA creates QR/NFC media kit identity
4. media kit is produced or manually fulfilled
5. owner submits proof
6. operator reviews proof
7. QR/NFC events are tracked
8. KPI reports are generated
9. payout eligibility is recorded
10. external rails settle money outside MEMBRA's proof layer

## Why Now

Local commerce is fragmented. Apartments, homes, vehicles, windows, bags, shirts, and local movement capacity are economically idle. QR/NFC, AI extraction, low-code deployment, Stripe-style payments, and proof workflows make it possible to structure this physical reality into auditable, permissioned economic events.

## Repository System

- `membra` — umbrella doctrine and ecosystem manifest
- `Membra_api` — canonical API gateway and shared object registry
- `Membra_mobile` — owner-side proof app shell
- `Membra_admin-` — operator review and decision console
- `Membra_wallet` — ledger and payout eligibility boundary
- `Membra_proofbook` — proof hash and audit ledger
- `Membra_contracts` — schemas, policies, Devnet-first proof anchors
- `Membra_ads` — physical proof-media campaign control plane
- `Membra_wear` — wearable QR/NFC media-kit generator
- `membra-relay` — local handoff and route-proof layer
- `membra-qr-gateway` — public QR/provenance gateway
- `Membra_kpi` — analytics and reporting engine
- `Membra_vendor_adapters` — non-executing vendor package registry
- `Membra_demo_data` — coherent demo universe
- `Membra_investor_room-` — diligence and buyer-ready room

## Product Promise

Every apartment can become the nearest warehouse, media surface, proof node, local handoff point, or neighborhood commerce participant — but only after consent, verification, proof, reporting, and payout eligibility review.

## Current State

MEMBRA is now structured as a multi-repo prototype ecosystem with runnable module scaffolds. It is not yet a mature operating company. Next steps are deployment, shared auth, unified database/event bus, hosted demos, screenshots, pilot users, and real proof-event data.

## Boundary Statement

MEMBRA records proof and eligibility. Proof is not money. Eligibility is not settlement. External regulated rails settle money.
